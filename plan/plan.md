## Primary actions (everywhere, unobtrusive)
- **WhatsApp** → opens chat with prefilled message: *"Hi, I found your salon website and would like to know more about your services."*
- **Call Now** → opens phone dialer
- **Get Directions** → opens the Google Maps location

- Floating WhatsApp button on desktop; a fixed bottom **Call | WhatsApp** bar on mobile.

## Sections (top to bottom)
1. **Navbar** — minimal, sticky, transparent-over-hero → solid on scroll; mobile drawer menu. Links: About · Services · Gallery · Reviews · Contact + **WhatsApp Us** CTA.
2. **Hero** — full-bleed real salon photo, eyebrow "PREMIUM BEAUTY & GROOMING", a bold serif headline, one supporting line, WhatsApp + Call CTAs. Signature on-load moment: headline revealed line-by-line with a masked reveal; subtle parallax on the hero image.
3. **Trust strip** — slim band: 4.6★ Rated · Unisex Salon · Premium Products · Relaxing Experience.
4. **About** — editorial layout, large real photo, short warm story, salon philosophy, a few supporting stats.
5. **Services** — Hair · Beauty · Makeup · Grooming, each with a short description. **No invented prices** (prices omitted unless you provide them). Numbered "manifesto chapter" styling.
6. **Why Choose Us** — 4–6 genuine strengths (experienced stylists, premium products, personalized service, clean hygienic space, modern techniques).
7. **Gallery** — editorial masonry grid of the real photos with hover zoom and a clean accessible lightbox.
8. **Testimonials** — layout built and ready; **you will paste 3–4 real reviews** to drop in. Until then it shows clearly-marked sample placeholders (name + review + rating) so nothing fake ships silently.
9. **Contact / Location** — strong closing CTA ("Ready for your next look?"), address, phone, daily hours, WhatsApp + Call + Get Directions, and an embedded Google map.
10. **Footer** — logo, short blurb, quick nav, services, address, phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, hours, copyright.

## Look & feel
- **Art direction:** one bold, cohesive editorial luxury direction pushed to the edge — not a template.
- **Palette:** restrained luxury — warm cream / soft beige backgrounds, deep charcoal text, a single muted gold–bronze accent used sparingly.
- **Typography:** elegant luxury serif for headings + clean modern sans for body/nav; typography does most of the visual work.
- **Motion (premium, purposeful):** smooth momentum scrolling, section fade-up reveals, gentle image zooms, one slow editorial marquee, a subtle hero parallax/3D moment, button & nav micro-interactions. Respects "reduce motion" settings.

## Explicitly NOT included
No accounts, auth, admin, appointment management, payments, database, CMS, or backend. Presentation + contact conversion only.

## Quality targets
Fast load, strong mobile-first design (not a shrunk desktop), accessible (keyboard nav, focus states, alt text, accessible menu & lightbox), and basic SEO (title, meta description, Open Graph, local-business info, semantic headings, sitemap, robots.txt).
